# Design Boundary Review

## Verdict
`ReviseDesign`

## Human Summary
The durable tagged models, append-only schema, guarded store primitives, corruption handling, history, and narrow foreign-key/reference seams belong to `workflowd-vs3.4.3`. The draft crosses into `workflowd-vs3.4.7` by assigning StageRun initialization, skip decisions, first-run activation, initial revision/work creation, and progression to WorkflowStart, and it describes neighboring handoff, reconciliation, publication, producer, Design-integration, and upgrade concerns too broadly unless they remain schema-only seams. Revise the Design to leave lifecycle orchestration with those named tickets while retaining the exact durable contracts and atomic primitives they require.
