# Design Boundary Review

## Verdict
`ScopeClean`

## Human Summary
The current Design keeps each material capability within `workflowd-vs3.4.3` or a narrow store and identity seam required by a named downstream ticket. Its atomic bootstrap and quarantine decisions resolve the prior contradictions without taking ownership of stage progression, producer or publication execution, reconciliation, handoff delivery, runtime composition, integrated verification, or offline legacy recovery. Keep the Design as written.
