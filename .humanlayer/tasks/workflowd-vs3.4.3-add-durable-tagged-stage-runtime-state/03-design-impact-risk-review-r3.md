## Human Summary

Design revision 3 resolves the earlier bootstrap and quarantine contradictions with an atomic `stage_snapshots_v1` to `stage_runtime_v1` boundary and a valid `StageRevision.abandoned`/`StageRun.data_error` terminal disposition. The remaining material hazards are covered by owned fail-closed controls, real-SQLite verification obligations, restart recovery, and an explicit before-exposure sequence for downstream legacy recovery. The Design is ready for the human Design gate; this verdict does not approve the Design or accept risk on the human's behalf.
